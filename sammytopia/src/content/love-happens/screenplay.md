🎬 LOVE HAPPENS

A Pratt Vision Studio Production

Written by Samuel Pratt

SCENE 1

EXT. UNIVERSITY CAMPUS – MORNING

Students walk around. Noise. Laughter.

Monique walks alone, earphones in.

Jason rides slowly on his bike.

He stops.

JASON

Monique! Monique!

She removes one earphone.

MONIQUE

Jason? Good morning.

JASON

Good morning. You’re quiet today.

MONIQUE

I’m always quiet.

JASON

No. Today is different.

She shrugs.

MONIQUE

Did you read for today’s test?

JASON

Honestly? No.

She smiles.

SCENE 2

INT. CLASSROOM – DAY

Teacher writes.

Monique stares at phone.

Jason whispers.

JASON

Put your phone down before sir catches you.

MONIQUE

I don’t care anymore.

JASON

Why?

MONIQUE

Life is stressful.

Teacher talks.

Monique stares at phone.

Jason writes notes for her.

He slides paper.

NOTE: Focus. I’ve got you.

She smiles.

Bell rings.

SCENE 3

EXT. CAMPUS BENCH – AFTERNOON

Monique sits, scrolling old pictures.

Jason walks up.

JASON

Hey, Monique.

MONIQUE

Hey.

JASON

How’s it going?

MONIQUE

Not great.

JASON

Why?

MONIQUE

Everything reminds me of him.

JASON

Who?

MONIQUE

My ex… Lamrana.

JASON

Oh…

(pause)

I’m sorry.

MONIQUE

Sometimes I feel stupid for loving him.

JASON

You’re not stupid. You’re human.

She looks at him.

MONIQUE

Thank you.

JASON

You don’t have to go through it alone.

MONIQUE

(smiles)

It’s nice having you.

JASON

Anytime.

Let me get you a bottle of water 

Monique 

Thank you.

Monique scrolling photos.

Jason sits with the bottle of water 

JASON

You still look at his pictures.

MONIQUE

I try not to.

JASON

But you do.

MONIQUE

Because I loved him.

JASON

And he lost you.

Pause.

MONIQUE

Why do you care so much?

JASON

Because I don’t like seeing you hurt.

SCENE 4

INT. HOSTEL ROOM – NIGHT

Monique on bed, crying.

Amina enters.

AMINA

Again?

MONIQUE

I miss him.

AMINA

He hurt you.

MONIQUE

I know.

AMINA

Then let him go.

Monique sobs

Monique still crying.

Amina comforts.

AMINA

Jason is always there.

MONIQUE

He deserves better than me.

SCENE 5

EXT. CAFETERIA – DAY

Jason and Kelvin eating.

KELVIN

You love her.

JASON

No.

KELVIN

Yes, you do.

JASON

Maybe… a little.

Jason rejected food.

JASON

I’m ok Just a bottle of water will do

KELVIN

You’re starving yourself.

JASON

My heart is tired.

KELVIN

Then fight for her.

SCENE 6

INT. LIBRARY – EVENING

Studying.

MONIQUE

Jason, explain this.

JASON

Okay… look here.

Hands touch.

Awkward.

They laugh.

SCENE 7

EXT. STREET – NIGHT

Walking home.

MONIQUE

Why are you always kind to me?

JASON

Because you deserve it.

She stops.

Looks at him.

MONIQUE

You’re special. Thank you for being my friend 

SCENE 8

INT. HOSTEL BALCONY – MORNING

Monique on phone.

MONIQUE

Hello?

LAMRANA (V.O.)

I miss you.

I’ve changed

Jason sees.

Heart breaks.

SCENE 9

EXT. FIELD – EVENING

Jason sits.

Kelvin joins.

JASON

I think she still loves him.

KELVIN

Then prove you love her more.

SCENE 10

INT. CAFETERIA – AFTERNOON

Monique quiet.

JASON

You’ve changed.

MONIQUE

I’m confused.

JASON

About what?

MONIQUE

About us.

I’m scared to love again.

Monique gets distracted by a wave 

JASON

Then let me teach you.

MONIQUE 

What did you say

JASON

Oh nothing  (he laughs)

SCENE 11

INT. CLASSROOM – DAY

Group work.

Amina and Kelvin joke.

Friendship grows.

SCENE 12

EXT. HOSTEL – EVENING

AMINA

Jason treats you better.

MONIQUE

I know.

AMINA

Then choose him.

SCENE 13

EXT. FIELD – SUNSET

Jason practices speech.

JASON

Monique, I love you…

No, no.

Again.

SCENE 14 (FLASHBACK)

EXT. PRIMARY SCHOOL – DAY

Young Jason holds flowers.

Boy runs past.

Girl smiles.

Jason drops flowers.

Cries.

SCENE 15

EXT. ROOFTOP – NIGHT

Jason and Monique.

JASON

I’m scared to lose you.

MONIQUE

You won’t.

JASON

Promise?

She holds his hand.

SCENE 16

EXT. CAMPUS GATE – DAY

Lamrana appears.

LAMRANA

Monique.

She freezes.

MONIQUE

Why are you here?

SCENE 17

INT. CLASSROOM – EVENING

Confrontation.

MONIQUE

You hurt me.

LAMRANA

I was stupid.

JASON

Let her go.

Silence.

SCENE 18 (FINAL)

EXT. GARDEN – SUNSET

Jason holds flowers.

Monique approaches.

JASON

Monique… I—

Her phone rings.

Lamrana.

She ignores it.

Looks at Jason.

MONIQUE

Say it.

Jason breathes.

JASON

I love you.

She smiles.

Cut to black.

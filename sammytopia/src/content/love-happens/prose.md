# LOVE HAPPENS
## A Story by Samuel Joshua Jason Pratt

### Chapter One — A Different Kind of Quiet

The university campus was alive with the ordinary noise of another morning. Students crossed the walkways, voices mixed with laughter, and somewhere nearby a class was already beginning.

Monique walked alone with her earphones in. Jason approached slowly on his bicycle and called her name.

“Monique! Monique!”

She removed one earphone and looked at him.

“Jason? Good morning.”

“Good morning. You’re quiet today.”

“I’m always quiet.”

“No. Today is different.”

She shrugged and changed the subject, asking whether he had studied for the day’s test. Jason admitted that he had not. She smiled, and for a moment the heaviness around her seemed to lift.

In class, however, Jason noticed that something was still wrong. Monique kept looking at her phone instead of following the lesson. He quietly warned her to put it away before the teacher noticed.

“I don’t care anymore,” she said.

“Why?”

“Life is stressful.”

Jason did not press her. Instead, he took notes for her and slid a small note across the desk: Focus. I’ve got you.

She smiled.

### Chapter Two — The Name Behind the Pain

That afternoon, Jason found Monique sitting on a campus bench, scrolling through old pictures on her phone. He asked how she was doing.

“Not great.”

“Why?”

“Everything reminds me of him.”

Jason hesitated. “Who?”

“My ex… Lamrana.”

Monique admitted that she sometimes felt stupid for still loving him. Jason told her she was not stupid; she was human. He reminded her that she did not have to go through the pain alone.

When he returned with a bottle of water, he noticed that she was still looking at Lamrana’s pictures.

“You still look at his pictures.”

“I try not to.”

“But you do.”

“Because I loved him.”

“And he lost you.”

She looked at him.

“Why do you care so much?”

“Because I don’t like seeing you hurt.”

Those words stayed with her.

That night in the hostel, Monique told Amina that she missed Lamrana. Amina reminded her that he had hurt her and encouraged her to let him go. She also pointed out that Jason was always there.

Monique looked away.

“He deserves better than me.”

### Chapter Three — A Feeling Jason Wouldn't Name

The next day, Jason sat with Kelvin at the cafeteria. Kelvin did not need long to understand what was happening.

“You love her.”

“No.”

“Yes, you do.”

Jason finally admitted, “Maybe… a little.”

He had little appetite. Kelvin noticed and challenged him to face what he was feeling rather than allowing it to consume him.

“My heart is tired,” Jason said.

“Then fight for her.”

Later, in the library, Monique asked Jason to explain something she did not understand. He leaned over her notes and patiently explained it. Their hands briefly touched. Both noticed. Neither knew what to say, so they laughed.

That small moment felt different.

Walking home later, Monique asked why Jason was always kind to her.

“Because you deserve it,” he said.

She stopped and looked at him.

“You’re special. Thank you for being my friend.”

Jason smiled, but the words were complicated by everything he was beginning to feel.

### Chapter Four — The Past Returns

The past returned through a phone call.

Monique answered on the hostel balcony. It was Lamrana. He told her that he missed her and that he had changed.

Jason happened to see enough to understand what the call meant.

His heart sank.

Later, sitting on the field with Kelvin, Jason admitted what he feared.

“I think she still loves him.”

Kelvin told him that if he truly cared for Monique, he should show her through his actions.

When Jason spoke to Monique again, he could tell she was confused.

“You’ve changed,” he said.

“I’m confused.”

“About what?”

“About us. I’m scared to love again.”

Jason wanted to tell her that he would not hurt her, but the words came out differently.

“Then let me teach you.”

“What did you say?”

“Oh, nothing,” he said with a nervous laugh.

### Chapter Five — Friends See What We Hide

Life continued around them. Amina and Kelvin became friends, and the group grew closer.

Amina noticed what Monique sometimes tried not to admit.

“Jason treats you better.”

“I know.”

“Then choose him.”

Monique did not answer immediately.

She knew Jason had become important to her, but choosing a new beginning meant confronting everything she still carried from the old one.

Meanwhile, Jason practised what he wanted to say. Alone on the field at sunset, he tried again and again.

“Monique, I love you…”

He stopped.

“No, no.”

He tried again.

### Chapter Six — The Fear of Losing Her

Jason's fear was not simply that Monique might choose Lamrana. It was that he might lose the friendship they had built.

On the rooftop one night, Jason finally allowed some of his fear to show.

“I’m scared to lose you.”

“You won’t,” Monique replied.

“Promise?”

She held his hand.

For Jason, the moment felt like hope—but hope could not erase the past.

A memory from his childhood returned to him: a young Jason holding flowers while a girl smiled nearby. The moment did not go as he imagined, and he remembered the embarrassment of letting the flowers fall.

Perhaps that was why saying what he felt had always frightened him.

### Chapter Seven — The Choice

At the campus gate, Lamrana appeared.

“Monique.”

She froze.

“Why are you here?”

The encounter reopened old questions. Lamrana insisted that he had changed. Monique had heard the promise before, and Jason was nearby, caught between wanting to protect her and knowing that the decision had to be hers.

The confrontation later became unavoidable.

“You hurt me,” Monique told Lamrana.

“I was stupid,” he replied.

Jason stepped in.

“Let her go.”

Silence followed.

Monique had to decide whether she would continue looking backward or allow herself to consider what had grown quietly in front of her.

### Chapter Eight — Love Happens

The garden was quiet at sunset.

Jason stood holding flowers as Monique approached.

He had rehearsed the moment many times, but now that she was actually there, the words seemed harder to find.

“Monique… I—”

Her phone rang.

Lamrana.

She looked at the screen and ignored the call.

Then she looked at Jason.

“Say it.”

Jason took a breath.

“I love you.”

Monique smiled.

The moment was simple. No grand speech was needed. After everything that had happened, the words had finally been said.

**THE END — SEASON 2 LOADING**
